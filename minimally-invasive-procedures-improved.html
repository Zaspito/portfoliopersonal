Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before minimally invasive care

Families deserve practical information before making a procedure decision. These answers explain what the approach can involve and why case selection matters.

What is minimally invasive veterinary surgery?

Minimally invasive veterinary surgery uses smaller incisions, natural openings, scopes, cameras, or advanced visualization systems to diagnose or treat selected conditions while reducing unnecessary tissue disruption.

For companion animals, this may include laparoscopy, endoscopy, arthroscopy, laser and energy-assisted procedures, or VITOM-assisted surgical visualization.

Which minimally invasive procedures are available?

Los Angeles Laparoscopy Vet offers laparoscopy, endoscopy, laser and energy procedures, arthroscopy, and VITOM surgical microscope support for selected cases.

Each procedure is used for different clinical needs, so the best next step is a consultation to determine which option is appropriate for your companion animal.

Is minimally invasive surgery right for every companion animal?

No. Minimally invasive surgery is recommended only when it is appropriate for the condition, anatomy, safety of the procedure, and expected outcome.

If another treatment option is safer or more effective, the team will explain the reasoning clearly before moving forward.

What areas do you serve?
